Deluge c1.3 MIDI presets

One MIDI preset per synth: loads the channel, all 16 gold-knob CC assignments, and links the device definition so CC names show on the OLED and in Automation View.

1 · CopyCopy the MIDI and MIDI_DEVICES folders from SD_CARD/ to the root of the Deluge SD card (merge with existing folders).
2 · Set the synthSet each synth to the MIDI channel on its card below and enable CC receive (see notes).
3 · LoadMake a MIDI track (press MIDI), then Load + MIDI, browse to the vendor folder, pick the synth, press Select.
4 · PlayPress one of the 8 mod buttons (left of the gold knobs) to pick a row below; turn the top/bottom gold knob. Press a gold knob to see its CC name.
5 · AutomateHold a pad/step + turn a knob for step automation, or record while playing. Automation View (press Clip while in the clip) lists only the labeled CCs.
6 · TweakReassign a knob: hold the gold knob + turn Select to pick any CC (names now show). Save the song or re-save the preset to keep it.

Definition: MIDI_DEVICES/DEFINITION/Elektron/Digitone.xml

  • Digitone: SETTINGS > MIDI CONFIG > CHANNELS: set the track you want to play to channel 5 (and RECEIVE CC/NRPN = On in MIDI CONFIG > SYNC/PORT CONFIG).
  • One preset = one Digitone track. For more tracks, duplicate the Deluge track and change its channel (6, 7, 8 ...) to match the other Digitone tracks.
  • The upstream Digitone file mixes track, FX and mixer CCs that reuse the same numbers; this one keeps the synth-track CCs only.
