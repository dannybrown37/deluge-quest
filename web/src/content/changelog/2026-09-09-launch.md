title: "deluge.quest goes live"
date: 2026-09-09
tag: feature
---

After working on the site for a while, [deluge.quest](https://deluge.quest) goes online with a collection of browser-based tools for the Synthstrom Deluge: card management, song stats, audio preview, score export, kit building, patch generation, MIDI import, and a backup CLI guide. Plus a music player showcasing Deluge-only tracks.

While the site is "public", it hasn't been posted anywhere yet. These tools are still being fine-tuned through personal usage on the web. The localhost testing could only provide so much confidence, this needed to go online.
