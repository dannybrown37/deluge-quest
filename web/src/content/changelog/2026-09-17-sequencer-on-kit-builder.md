---
title: "Kit Builder gets a sequencer"
date: 2026-09-17
tag: feature
---

I love the Vim-like keyboard driving on the Kit Builder, but I haven't used it as much as I'd like
since it's hard to test how the *kit* sounds to date, you know? It's possible to audition the
individual sounds, but not to make a sequence that helps one understand how the sounds work
*together*.

That is no longer true! The Kit Builder now has a 16-step sequencer. It's very basic, but it allows
you to validate that the kit has the sounds you need before you save. It does not have the ability
to save the *song*, as one measure on the Deluge isn't really a tall order for a user.
